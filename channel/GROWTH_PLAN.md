## 5. Purani 54 posts: **ARCHIVE karo, delete nahi**

- Archive wapas laaya ja sakta hai, delete nahi.
- Na Instagram ne, na Mosseri ne kabhi kaha hai ki delete karne se reach badhti hai. Mosseri ki salah "hide, don't delete" batayi jaati hai.
- **Archive karo:** saari off-topic posts.
  - Call recordings (**phone number wali sabse pehle**)
  - Protest text, AI tips (agar movie niche chuna)
  - Doosron ke movie clips jaise ke taise (Saiyaara etc.), kyunki ye repost/originality risk hain
  - Safed thumbnail wali post (ya uska cover theek karo)
- **Rakho:** sirf wo jo naye niche me fit ho aur aapka banaya hua ho (KING decode).
- Kaise: post → ⋯ → Archive. Grid saaf dikhna chahiye, taaki naya visitor 3 second me samajh jaaye ki account kis baare me hai.

## 7. Har reel ke rules (Instagram ke ranking signals ke hisaab se)

Mosseri ke mutabik ranking ke top 3 signals hain: **watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach.** Non-followers ke liye *sends* (DM me bhejna) zyada important hai. Insights me ab **Skip rate** bhi hai (kitne log pehle 3 second me chale gaye).

| Rule | Kyun |
|---|---|
| **30–60 sec**, max 90 sec. 3 min se upar kabhi nahi (discovery ke liye) | Watch time %, aur 3 min se lambe reels non-followers tak nahi jaate |
| **Pehle 2 sec me hook** + subject screen par ("Romantic gaana… aur 30 sec me goli!") | Skip rate kam |
| **Ek reel = ek topic** | Poora dekhte hain |
| Ant me **send CTA**: "Ye us dost ko bhejo jo SRK ka fan hai" | Sends per reach |
| Ant me **do-option sawaal**: "Hit ya flop? Comment karo" | Comments + loop |
| **Cover hamesha set karo** (skill `cover_9x16.jpg` deti hai) | Grid click |
| **Captions on-screen** (skill deti hai) | Bina awaaz ke dekhne wale |
| Caption ki pehli line = hook + keywords (film ka naam) | Search |
| **3–5 hashtags** (Dec 2025 se limit 5 ki khabar hai, pakka nahi) | Spam na lage |
| `_no_music.mp4` + Instagram ka **trending audio 10–15% volume** par | Audio trend ka fayda |
| **Raw clip, dusre ka watermark, ya bina badlav ka repost: kabhi nahi** | Originality policy |
| Apni **asli awaaz** record karo (skill Whisper se sync karti hai) | Free TTS se behtar retention (ye meri salah hai, data nahi) |

## 10. AI (Claude Code) ko kya bolna hai: copy-paste prompts

### Prompt A: roz ka topic research (subah)
```
Skill activate karo. Aaj [DATE] hai. India ke Bollywood + OTT me pichhle 24–48 ghante ke
top 5 trending topics web par research karo:
naye trailer/teaser/gaane, box office numbers, OTT releases, badi khabar ya controversy.
Har topic ke liye do:
1) kyun trending hai + source links (sirf bharosemand sites),
2) views/search ka jo bhi number mile (source ke saath; nahi mile to "nahi mila" likho),
3) 2-second hook ka idea,
4) kaunsa pillar (Decode / Numbers / Facts / Is hafte / Theory).
Phir sabse zyada viral chance wala 1 topic chuno aur 3 line me wajah batao.
Kuch bhi guess mat karna. Jo verify na ho, use "unverified" likho.
```

### Prompt B: script + video (topic chunne ke baad)
```
Topic: [TOPIC]. Is par 45–60 second ka 9:16 Hindi (Hinglish captions) reel banao.
- Pehle script MD do: hook pehle 2 sec me, kul 110–140 words, har fact ke saath source,
  ant me "ye us dost ko bhejo jo..." + do-option comment sawaal.
- Media: meri di hui images [media/ folder] + Wikimedia Commons (credits ke saath).
  Raw movie clip ya kisi aur ka watermark mat use karna.
- Awaaz: [meri recording: media/voice.m4a] YA hindi_male.
- Proof banao, frames khud dekho, kamiyan theek karo, phir make.
  QA PASS aur review.md ke baad hi deliver.
- Mujhe do: final mp4, _no_music.mp4, cover_9x16.jpg, caption (pehli line = hook + film ka naam),
  3–5 hashtags.
```

### Prompt C: weekly review (har Tuesday)
```
Ye mere pichhle 7 reels ke Insights screenshots hain
(views, skip rate, average watch time, sends, follows).
Table banao: kaunsa hook/pillar/length sabse achha chala aur kaunsa nahi, aur kyun.
Agle 7 din ka plan badlo: kaunsa format zyada, kaunsa band, posting time sahi hai ya nahi.
Numbers sirf screenshots se lena, guess nahi.
```

### Prompt D: hit reel ko aur kaam me lao
```
Ye reel [link/file] sabse achha chala. Isi format me 3 naye topics research karo
aur iska "Part 2" script likho. Saath me iska 30 sec version naye hook ke saath banao.
```

### Prompt E: lambi video ko chhote reels me kaato
```
Ye 3 min ki video/project [path] hai. Isse 3 alag 30–45 sec reels banao.
Har reel ka apna 2-sec hook ho aur apna comment sawaal ho.
```

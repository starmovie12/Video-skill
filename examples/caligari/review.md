# Self-review — caligari

Reviewed by opening `contact_sheet.jpg` and the per-scene frames of the final render.

## What works
- Hook: the poster card slams into frame 1, then '106 saal purani film… aur aaj bhi daravni' lands word by word. A clear pattern-interrupt, not a greeting.
- 9 scenes, 9 different layouts (poster punch, framed title, story cards with poster thumbnails, cast portraits, pros/cons glass cards, kinetic quote, rating ring, stamp verdict, CTA loop).
- The palette comes from the poster (magenta-orange), so it feels like *this* film.

## Weaknesses
- Mostly one colour family (dark magenta/brown). The duotone background plates are low-contrast and a bit muddy.
- The cast scene uses monograms (WK / CV) because the poster only shows Caligari. That is honest, but less visual than real actor stills.
- The poster card in the hook is fairly small at the top; it could take more of the frame in the first second.
- The music is procedural (epic_trailer): fine as a bed, not a real trailer score.

## Next time
- Fix the weaknesses above before the next video in this category.

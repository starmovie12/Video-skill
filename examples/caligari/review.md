# Self-review — caligari

Reviewed by opening the contact sheet and per-scene frames of the final render (v2 engine).

## What works
- Real public-domain film stills fill every scene (9/9 on footage): Cesare's face in the hook, the painted streets behind the story, the rooftop behind pros/cons.
- Cast cards show the RIGHT faces (Krauss and Veidt cropped from one publicity still), not monograms.
- 😱 sticker on the hook, film-perforation motif, glow captions, 3 cuts snapped to the music beat.

## Weaknesses
- The stills are black-and-white, so the whole video is one grey/orange family; a colour accent beyond salmon would help.
- The story-card thumbnails are poster crops (same Caligari face twice); stills of the characters would read better.
- The poster title card has no footage cut inside it, so it feels static next to the faster scenes.
- The music is procedural (epic_trailer). For posting, the no-music version + a trending audio is probably stronger.

## Next time
- Fix the weaknesses above before the next video in this category.
